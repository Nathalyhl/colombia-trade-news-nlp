# Research status · version 1.0

The frozen primary frame has 1011 provisionally eligible press articles in all 80 publication months, March 2020–October 2026. Both primary domains appear in 78 months. The complete archive contains 3517 candidates, 2868 extracted texts and 1423 eligible deduplicated articles, including complementary institutional/legacy sources. The earlier Analdex-only historical limitation has been addressed through direct publisher sitemap acquisition; representativeness is not established.

Executed: source/date/body audit; transparent scope and document-role proposals; fixed-model full-body predictions for 1423 eligible texts; lexical equations; coverage-based period selection; an independent 200-record evaluation packet; a read-only audit of the supplied 309-month export workbook; descriptive and retrospective economic comparisons with retained failures and diagnostics. The main tone RMSE is 8.593, versus 8.741 for its coverage-controlled base, over twelve months. Adverse residual/stationarity diagnostics prevent a validated-prediction claim.

Not completed: independent human judgments (zero adjudicated labels), classification/country accuracy, authenticated historical economic vintages/releases, historically available text/model versions, and a diagnostically adequate predictor evaluated on fresh temporal data. No generated gold labels, fabricated source text, official endorsement or exact central-bank experiment replication is claimed.
