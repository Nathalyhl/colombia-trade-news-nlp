# Research brief

The project asks three separate questions: is the recovered corpus relevant to Colombian goods exports; do the linguistic instruments measure their stated targets; and does lagged news information improve an export-history comparator? Acquisition, annotation and economic utility require different evidence.

The fixed primary press frame spans March 2020–October 2026, with direct publisher sitemap lineage from Valora Analitik and La República. Institutional Analdex documents and other acquisition routes remain complementary. Period selection uses coverage only. Statistical-report and event/expectation cues remain proposals until independently reviewed.

BE1340 contributes a published lexical instrument and the rationale for comparing economic models with/without news. The Ecuador study informs direct extraction, Spanish contextual measurement and independent evaluation. Public PySentimiento code/weights are credited separately. Exact original author scripts and experiments are not claimed.

The executed twelve-origin export comparison is retrospective, uses an assumed publication calendar and current numerical history, and retains adverse diagnostics. A small RMSE reduction does not establish a validated predictor. The 200-record independent human sample is locked and blank; software tests and model probabilities cannot replace its judgments. See [Spanish protocol](STUDY_PROTOCOL_ES.md), [economic findings](ECONOMIC_RESULTS_ES.md) and [status](PROJECT_STATUS.md) for the exact scope and next evidentiary requirements.
